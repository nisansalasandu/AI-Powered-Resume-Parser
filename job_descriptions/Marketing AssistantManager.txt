Marketing Assistant/Manager
The marketing Assistant Manager supports the planning and execution of marketing campaigns, assists in brand development, and analyzes market trends to improve business growth. 
Key Responsibilities:
•	Assist in developing and executing marketing strategies.
•	Coordinate digital and offline marketing campaigns.
•	Analyze campaign performance and prepare reports.
•	Support campaign creation and social media reports.
Requirements:
•	Bachelor’s degree in Marketing or related field.
•	2-4 years of experience in marketing.
•	Knowledge of digital marketing tools and analytics.
