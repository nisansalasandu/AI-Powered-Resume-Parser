Accounts Executive
The Account Executive is responsible for handling daily accounting operations, maintaining financial records, and supporting financial reporting and compliance activities.
Key Responsibilities:
•	Maintain accurate accounting records and documentation.
•	Prepare invoices, vouchers, and payment schedules.
•	Assist in monthly and annual financial reporting.
•	Coordinate with auditors and internal teams.
Requirements:
•	Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance or related fields.
•	1-3 years of experience in accounting or finance.
