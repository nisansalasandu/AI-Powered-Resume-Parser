Human Resource Manager
The human resource manager is responsible for overseeing all HR functions including recruitment, employee relations, performance management and compliance with labor regulations. 
Key Responsibilities:
•	Manage end-to-end recruitment and onboarding processes.
•	 Develop and implement HR policies and procedures.
•	Handle employee relations and performance management.
•	Ensure compliance with labor laws and company regulations.
Requirements:
•	Bachelor's degree with 3-5 years' experience in HR operations or related.
•	3-5 years of experience in HR operations.
•	Strong communication and leadership skills.
